> *"Some people don't stay beside you all the time. Sometimes they stay inside the places they touched."*

## 📖 The Story: The Firefly Diary

### The Forest Born from Memories
Long ago, this forest didn't exist. It was created by a single diary. The diary belonged to someone who wrote down whenever something made them smile—tiny moments like someone asking if they had eaten, someone losing a game, someone saying, *"There's always tomorrow."* Every memory became a firefly.

Then life became louder. Conversations became shorter. *"Tomorrow"* quietly replaced *"Today."* The fireflies started fading, and the diary scattered.

You wake up as a small glowing forest spirit carrying an iron lantern. Your job isn't saving the forest. Your job is **remembering**.

---

## 🌲 The 10 Memory Pages Scattered Across the Woods

1. **Tiny Coffee Mug**: *"One coffee became two. Then four. Then somehow... nobody remembered who owed whom anymore."*
2. **Old Arcade Cabinet**: *"The high score was never important. Laughing because somebody cheated was."*
3. **Football Under Tree**: *"Practice finished. Boots removed. Somebody was still waiting."*
4. **Cauliflower Flower**: *"Not every flower needs petals. 😂"*
5. **Notebook Page**: *"Let's learn together."*
6. **Empty Rain Bench**: *"Today's slot expired. There's always tomorrow."*
7. **Tiny Chocolate**: *"Someone chose only chocolate. Someone forced them to choose a book too."*
8. **Motivational Book (The Shared Dream)**: *"Somehow... they both picked the same one."*
9. **Glowing Pen**: *"Nobody knew whose pen it really was. 😂"*
10. **Tiny Lantern**: *"Some stories are easier to tell in dreams."*

## 🌿 Dynamic World Transformations (The Forest Remembers)

As you gather memory pages, the forest itself responds and comes alive:
- ☕ **Coffee Memory** → A cozy campfire sparks to life by the Coffee Kiosk.
- ⚽ **Football Memory** → A scuffed leather football appears in the Memory Hollow grass.
- 📄 **Notebook Memory** → Swirling paper pages float softly through the Central Glade.
- 🍫 **Chocolate Memory** → A cute squirrel appears under the Observatory tree nibbling chocolate.
- 🥦 **Cauliflower Memory** → A vibrant yellow flower patch blooms around the Sunset Bench.
- 🌙 **The Dream Memory** → Firefly density across the entire forest doubles!
